VidCraft Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 2026-07-06

1. Who We Are

This Privacy Policy applies to the VidCraft mobile application (the "App"). We value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This policy explains how we collect, use, store, share, and protect your information, and the rights you may exercise.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

  • Account information: when you sign in via third-party providers (such as Google), we may receive basic profile details authorized by you (for example, nickname, avatar, and account identifier).
  • User input: links, text, search queries, editing parameters, and other content you submit in the App.

2.2 Device and Log Information

  • Device information: model, operating system version, app version, language, time zone, and related technical identifiers.
  • Operational logs: crash logs, performance metrics, error diagnostics, and network status for stability and troubleshooting.

2.3 Permission-Related Information

The App may request the following system permissions (depending on your device and feature usage):

Permission Purpose Can You Deny?
Photos/Media Read & Write To save images/videos to your gallery or import local media for editing. Yes. Related import/save features will not work if denied.
Camera (if used) To capture photos/videos as source material. Yes. Camera features will not work if denied.
Network Access To parse links, load previews, authenticate sign-in, and deliver online functionality. Required for online features. Disabling network access limits functionality.
Clipboard Read (user-triggered) To detect and parse links from clipboard when you initiate the action. Yes. You can avoid triggering this feature.

3. How We Use Information

  • To provide core features such as link parsing, preview, download/save, and image editing.
  • To support sign-in, account security, and abuse prevention.
  • To improve product quality through diagnostics and performance analysis.
  • To comply with legal obligations and platform requirements.

4. Third-Party Services and Sharing

To provide certain features, the App may integrate third-party services (for example Firebase, Google Sign-In, WebView content loading, and link parsing services). These providers may process data within the scope necessary to deliver their services. We do not sell your personal information.

5. Data Storage and Security

  • We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or loss.
  • We retain information only as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy, unless a longer retention period is required by law.

6. Your Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have the right to:

  • Access, correct, or delete your personal data.
  • Withdraw consent (for example, app permissions or third-party authorization).
  • Request account deletion (if account functionality is available).
  • Contact us with privacy questions, concerns, or complaints.

7. Children's Privacy

If you are under the age required by your local law, please use the App under the guidance of a parent or legal guardian. We do not knowingly collect unnecessary personal data from children.

8. Cross-Border Data Transfers

Because certain third-party services may be hosted in different regions, your data may be processed outside your country or region. We implement safeguards as required by applicable law.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time due to business, legal, or technical changes. Material updates will be notified through in-app notice or other appropriate means.
